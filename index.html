What this is

A search-engine-style dashboard for learning value investing: pick a company, read its 20-year story in plain language, and see exactly where every number came from.

What this deliberately isn't

No trading signals, no price targets, no buy/sell buttons, no brokerage hookup. Every formula is hidden by default behind a "How we got this" drawer — this is a research tool, not investment advice.

How it stays free

Every figure is fetched on the fly, in your browser, from free public filing sources — the SEC's EDGAR API for US companies, UK Companies House for a curated list of LSE names. Nothing is cached on a server or database; close the tab and there's nothing left behind.
